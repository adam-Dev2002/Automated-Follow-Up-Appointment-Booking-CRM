    Reminders for business owners and clients

    Business owners manage the schedule and follow up on changes. Clients review their appointment details and reply to the business when they need help.

    Business owner reminder email

    Save your Gmail address as the default for your business. Clients receive their appointment reminders, and you receive a private copy 24 hours and 1 hour before eligible appointments.

    This address receives reminders; emails are sent from the business?s connected Gmail account. Leave the address blank to stop owner copies.

    Loading reminder settings?

    1. Start your workspace. Keep n8n and the dashboard server running. Use Check connection to confirm the booking API is available.
    2. Create a booking. Click New appointment, enter the customer’s name, email and service, then choose the date, time and duration. Times use Asia/Manila.
    3. Review the result. The workflow checks for overlapping appointments. A successful booking is saved and its confirmation is queued.
    4. Prepare for each appointment. Review your upcoming bookings and calendar, plan the service, and stay in touch with your client. Open the appointment details or click Refresh delivery. Review the confirmation, 24-hour reminder and 1-hour reminder results.
    5. Handle changes. Open a booking’s details to reschedule or cancel before it starts. For a record from another browser, use Manage by reference with the reference and private management code.
    6. Read customer replies. Customers can reply to the appointment email. Staff reviews the reply and makes the change in the dashboard.
    Remember: the list shows bookings saved in this browser. Keep n8n running so scheduled follow-ups can be processed.
    1. Request an appointment. Contact the business with your name, email, requested service and preferred time.
    2. Review your confirmation. Check the highlighted service, date, time and booking reference. All times are in Asia/Manila.
    3. Stay ready for your appointment. Save the booking reference and private management code. Eligible appointments receive reminders 24 hours and 1 hour before the start.
    4. Request a change by replying. Include your booking reference and preferred new time, or state that you want to cancel. The business will review and update your booking.
    5. Make changes before the start. This workflow does not allow cancellation or rescheduling after the appointment begins.

    Customer self-booking from a public page is not available in this local version. Staff enters and manages bookings for customers.
